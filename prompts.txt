=== Prompt 5.3: Precision@K and Recall@K ===
Date: [Date]

"Implement Precision@K, Recall@K, and F1@K metrics for ranking evaluation.

Response Summary:
[Precision@K: relevant in top K / K, Recall@K: relevant in top K / total 
relevant, F1@K: harmonic mean of both]
